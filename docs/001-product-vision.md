# 1. Vision Statement

Nebula ERP is a modern, modular, AI-ready Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform designed to simplify business operations for organizations of all sizes. It combines inventory management, sales, purchasing, accounting, customer relationship management, service management, and business intelligence into a single, intuitive platform.

Nebula ERP is built with a cloud-first, API-first, and user-first philosophy, focusing on speed, reliability, scalability, and an exceptional user experience.

The long-term vision is to create a business operating system that grows with an organization—from a single retail shop to a multi-branch enterprise.

---

# 2. Mission

Nebula ERP aims to provide businesses with a single, integrated platform to manage their daily operations efficiently. By combining modern technology, intuitive design, and enterprise-grade architecture, Nebula ERP reduces operational complexity, improves productivity, and enables data-driven decision-making.

The platform is designed to be modular, scalable, and adaptable, allowing businesses to start with essential modules and expand as their operational needs grow.

---

# 3. Product Goals

## Primary Goals

- Provide a complete, modern, and scalable ERP platform.
- Deliver a fast, intuitive, and user-friendly experience.
- Support businesses from single-location stores to multi-branch enterprises.
- Integrate all core business operations into one system.
- Provide powerful reporting and business insights.
- Ensure enterprise-grade security, reliability, and auditability.

## Long-Term Goals

- Become the preferred ERP platform for SMEs and growing enterprises.
- Support cloud, self-hosted, and hybrid deployments.
- Provide AI-powered automation and analytics.
- Build a modular ecosystem that allows businesses to enable only the features they need.
- Establish Nebula ERP as a complete Business Operating System.

# 5. Core Product Principles

Nebula ERP is built upon the following principles, which guide every design and engineering decision.

1. **User First** – Every feature must solve a real business problem.
2. **Simple by Default** – Powerful functionality should remain easy to use.
3. **Modular Architecture** – Businesses should install only the modules they need.
4. **Performance Matters** – Speed is a core feature, not an afterthought.
5. **Security by Design** – Protect business data through secure development practices.
6. **AI Ready** – Every module should be designed with future AI integration in mind.
7. **API First** – All major business functions should be accessible through well-designed APIs.
8. **Cloud Native** – Designed for cloud deployment while supporting self-hosted environments.
9. **Scalable** – Capable of supporting both small businesses and enterprise organizations.
10. **Consistent Experience** – Every module should follow the same design language and user experience.

# 6. Success Metrics

The success of Nebula ERP will be measured using the following objectives.

## Product Metrics

- High system reliability and uptime.
- Fast application response times.
- Consistent user experience across all modules.
- Modular expansion without affecting existing functionality.

## Business Metrics

- Reduced business operation time through automation.
- Improved inventory accuracy.
- Faster sales and purchase processing.
- Better financial visibility through reporting and analytics.
- Increased customer satisfaction through an intuitive user experience.

## Technical Metrics

- Scalable architecture capable of supporting multiple businesses and branches.
- Secure authentication and authorization.
- Maintainable and well-documented codebase.
- Comprehensive API coverage for integrations.
- Automated testing for critical business functionality.

# 7. Out of Scope (Version 1.0)

The following capabilities are intentionally excluded from the initial release of Nebula ERP and may be considered for future versions.

- Manufacturing Resource Planning (MRP)
- Human Resource Management (HRM)
- Payroll Processing
- E-Commerce Marketplace
- Learning Management System (LMS)
- Hospital Management
- School Management
- Hotel Management
- Advanced AI Decision Engine
- IoT Device Management
- Native Mobile Applications
- Multi-Tenant SaaS Platform

These features are planned as future modules or editions and are not required for the successful delivery of Nebula ERP Version 1.0.

---

# 8. Product Summary

Nebula ERP is designed to become a complete Business Operating System for modern organizations. By combining enterprise-grade architecture with a clean user experience and a modular design philosophy, the platform enables businesses to manage operations from a single unified system.

Version 1.0 focuses on delivering a robust foundation that includes authentication, organization management, user roles and permissions, products, inventory, purchasing, sales, POS, warehouse management, accounting, CRM, reporting, repair management, warranty management, and serial number tracking.

Future versions will expand the platform with additional industry-specific modules, AI-powered capabilities, mobile applications, and advanced integrations while maintaining backward compatibility and a consistent user experience.
