# 1. Purpose

The Audit Log module provides a centralized, immutable record of user actions, system events, configuration changes, authentication activities, API requests, and data access across the Nebula ERP platform.

It enables organizations to meet security, compliance, operational, and forensic requirements by maintaining complete historical records of important events while integrating with every ERP module.

The Audit Log serves as the authoritative source for accountability and traceability.

# 12. Business Rules

The Audit Log module enforces the following rules.

## BR-001

Every audit record belongs to exactly one organization.

Audit data is completely isolated between organizations.

---

## BR-002

Audit records are immutable.

Once written, an audit record cannot be modified or deleted through standard application operations.

Any administrative archival process must preserve record integrity and chain of custody.

---

## BR-003

Every significant business event shall generate an audit record automatically.

Manual audit creation is not permitted except for approved system migration or recovery procedures.

---

## BR-004

Audit logging failures shall never interrupt successful business transactions unless organization policy explicitly requires strict audit enforcement.

Failures shall be logged for administrative investigation.

---

## BR-005

Sensitive information shall never be stored in audit records.

Examples include:

- Passwords
- Authentication Secrets
- Private Keys
- Access Tokens
- OTP Codes
- Payment Card Data
- Encryption Keys

Only masked or metadata representations may be stored where required.

---

## BR-006

Every audit record shall include sufficient contextual information to reconstruct the performed action.

This includes:

- User
- Organization
- Module
- Entity
- Action
- Timestamp

Additional metadata should be recorded where available.

---

## BR-007

Archived audit logs remain searchable according to organizational retention policy.

Archived records remain read-only.

---

## BR-008

System-generated actions shall be clearly identified as automated events.

Automated actions should record:

- Service Name
- Background Job
- Integration
- Scheduler
- AI Service (Future)

instead of a human user.

---

## BR-009

Exported audit reports shall preserve chronological order and include export metadata.

Each export records:

- Export Time
- Exported By
- Applied Filters
- Export Format

---

## BR-010

Every audit query performed by privileged users may itself be audited according to organization policy.
