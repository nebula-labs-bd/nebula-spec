# 1. Purpose

The Point of Sale (POS) module provides a fast, reliable, and user-friendly checkout system for retail, wholesale, restaurants, pharmacies, supermarkets, and service businesses.

It enables real-time sales processing while synchronizing inventory, payments, customers, accounting, and reporting across the ERP platform.

The POS is designed to operate efficiently even in high-volume environments with optional offline capability.

# 12. Business Rules

The POS module enforces the following rules.

## BR-001

Every POS transaction belongs to exactly one organization, one branch, and one registered POS terminal.

All transactions are isolated between organizations.

---

## BR-002

A cashier must have an active shift before processing any transaction.

Transactions cannot be created outside an active shift unless explicitly permitted by organization policy.

---

## BR-003

Only active products with available pricing may be sold.

Products marked as inactive, archived, or unavailable cannot be added to the shopping cart.

---

## BR-004

Inventory is updated immediately after a successful sale unless the organization has enabled deferred inventory synchronization.

---

## BR-005

Returns and exchanges must reference a previous completed sale unless organization policy allows returns without receipts.

---

## BR-006

Discount permissions follow role-based authorization.

Supported discount policies:

- No Manual Discount
- Cashier Discount Limit
- Manager Approval Required
- Unlimited

---

## BR-007

Mixed payment transactions must equal the invoice total before completion.

The system shall reject transactions with underpayment or overpayment beyond configured tolerance.

---

## BR-008

Offline transactions are synchronized automatically after connectivity is restored.

Duplicate synchronization must never create duplicate sales.

---

## BR-009

Completed sales are immutable.

Corrections require refunds, exchanges, or return transactions.

---

## BR-010

Every completed transaction generates inventory, accounting, payment, and audit records automatically.

# 15. Offline Mode

Nebula POS supports optional offline operation.

Offline mode allows:

- Product Search
- Barcode Scanning
- Sales Processing
- Receipt Printing
- Customer Selection (Cached)
- Cash Payments
- Limited Card Payments (Subject to Payment Provider)

During offline operation:

- Transactions are stored locally.
- Inventory changes are queued.
- Synchronization occurs automatically when connectivity returns.
- Conflict resolution follows organization-defined synchronization policies.

# 25. Error Scenarios

| Scenario | Expected Result |
|----------|-----------------|
| No active shift | Transaction rejected |
| Invalid barcode | Product not found |
| Product unavailable | Validation error |
| Payment mismatch | Transaction rejected |
| Terminal offline during online-only payment | Payment rejected |
| Duplicate offline synchronization | Duplicate transaction prevented |
| Unauthorized refund | 403 Forbidden |
| Cash drawer variance exceeds policy | Manager approval required |
| Receipt not found | 404 Not Found |
| Shift already closed | Operation rejected |

Error messages should clearly describe the issue while avoiding disclosure of internal implementation details.
