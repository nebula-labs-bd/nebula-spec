# 1. Purpose

The Accounting module serves as the financial backbone of Nebula ERP.

It manages the organization's financial records through double-entry bookkeeping, supporting the Chart of Accounts, journal entries, general ledger, accounts receivable, accounts payable, fiscal periods, tax management, budgeting, cost centers, financial reporting, and period closing.

Every financial transaction generated by other ERP modules ultimately affects this module.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Accounting module must:

- Support double-entry accounting
- Maintain the Chart of Accounts

- Support multi-currency accounting
- Support tax management

# 8. Chart of Accounts (CoA)

The Chart of Accounts defines every financial account used within the organization.

Each account stores:

- Account Code
- Account Name
- Parent Account
- Account Type
- Account Category
- Currency
- Cost Center Required
- Status

Supported account types:

- Asset
- Liability
- Equity
- Revenue
- Expense

Accounts may be organized in a hierarchical structure.

Inactive accounts cannot receive new postings.

---

# 9. Fiscal Years & Accounting Periods

The Accounting module supports configurable fiscal calendars.

Each Fiscal Year stores:

- Fiscal Year Name
- Start Date
- End Date
- Status

Each Accounting Period stores:

- Period Name
- Month
- Start Date
- End Date
- Status

Supported statuses:

- Open
- Closed
- Locked

Only open periods may receive new journal entries.

# 10. Journal Entries

Journal Entries record financial transactions using double-entry accounting.

Each Journal Entry stores:

- Journal Number
- Entry Date
- Fiscal Period
- Description
- Currency
- Exchange Rate
- Debit Accounts
- Credit Accounts
- Total Debit
- Total Credit
- Approval Status
- Posting Status

Supported statuses:

- Draft
- Pending Approval
- Approved
- Posted
- Reversed

Total Debits must always equal Total Credits.

---

# 11. General Ledger

The General Ledger maintains the financial history of every account.

Each ledger transaction stores:

- Journal Reference
- Account
- Debit
- Credit
- Running Balance
- Fiscal Period
- Cost Center
- Branch
- Currency
- Exchange Rate
- Posting Date

The General Ledger is generated automatically from posted journal entries and serves as the source for financial reporting.

# 12. Business Rules

The Accounting module enforces the following rules.

## BR-001

Every accounting record belongs to exactly one organization.

Financial data is completely isolated between organizations.

---

## BR-002

Every posted Journal Entry must satisfy double-entry accounting principles.

Total Debits must always equal Total Credits.

Unbalanced journal entries cannot be approved or posted.

---

## BR-003

Journal Entries may only be posted into Open accounting periods.

Closed or Locked periods reject new postings unless reopened by authorized users.

---

## BR-004

Posted Journal Entries are immutable.

Corrections require:

- Reversing Journal Entry
- Adjustment Journal Entry

Direct modification of posted entries is prohibited.

---

## BR-005

Every Journal Entry must reference at least one valid Chart of Accounts account.

Inactive or archived accounts cannot receive new postings.

---

## BR-006

Multi-currency transactions shall store:

- Transaction Currency
- Base Currency
- Exchange Rate
- Converted Amount

Historical exchange rates remain unchanged after posting.

---

## BR-007

Subledger balances must reconcile with the General Ledger.

This applies to:

- Accounts Receivable
- Accounts Payable

System reconciliation should be available at any time.

---

## BR-008

Taxes are calculated according to the organization's configured tax rules.

Manual tax overrides require appropriate authorization.

---

## BR-009

Budget validation may occur before posting expenses when enabled by organization policy.

Budget warnings or blocking behavior is configurable.

---

## BR-010

Financial closing locks accounting periods from further postings until reopened by authorized personnel.

# 18. Validation Rules

| Field | Validation |
|--------|------------|
| Account | Required, active account |
| Fiscal Period | Must be Open |
| Debit Amount | Zero or greater |
| Credit Amount | Zero or greater |
| Total Debits | Must equal Total Credits |
| Currency | Valid ISO currency code |
| Exchange Rate | Greater than zero for foreign currency |
| Tax Code | Required where applicable |
| Cost Center | Required if account configuration requires it |

Validation must occur on both the client and server.
