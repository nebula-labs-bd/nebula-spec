# 1. Purpose

The CRM module manages customer relationships throughout the entire customer lifecycle, from lead generation to long-term customer retention.

It supports leads, contacts, accounts, opportunities, activities, follow-ups, pipelines, communication history, forecasting, marketing integration, and customer interaction tracking while integrating with Sales, Customers, Reports, Notifications, and future AI services.

The CRM module provides a centralized view of every customer interaction.

# 12. Business Rules

The CRM module enforces the following rules.

## BR-001

Every CRM record belongs to exactly one organization.

CRM data is completely isolated between organizations.

---

## BR-002

Every Lead must have at least one contact method.

Supported contact methods include:

- Email
- Phone
- Mobile

Organizations may require additional contact information according to policy.

---

## BR-003

A Lead may only be converted once.

Lead conversion creates:

- Customer Account (if required)
- Contact
- Opportunity

Original Lead records remain preserved for historical reference.

---

## BR-004

Every Opportunity must belong to one Account.

Multiple Opportunities may exist for the same Account.

---

## BR-005

Pipeline stages follow the organization's configured sales workflow.

Users cannot skip mandatory stages unless authorized by organization policy.

---

## BR-006

Activities, Tasks, Calls, Meetings, Emails, and Notes are permanently linked to their related Lead, Contact, Account, or Opportunity.

Deleting a parent record must not remove historical activity records.

---

## BR-007

Completed Tasks are immutable.

Corrections require reopening the task or creating a new follow-up task.

---

## BR-008

Closed Opportunities (Won or Lost) cannot return to active pipeline stages unless reopened by authorized users.

---

## BR-009

Communication history is append-only.

Historical customer interactions cannot be modified after recording except by authorized administrators.

---

## BR-010

Every CRM action automatically updates the customer interaction timeline and generates audit records.

# 17. Validation Rules

| Field | Validation |
|--------|------------|
| Lead Name | Required |
| Account | Required for Opportunity |
| Contact | Required where applicable |
| Assigned User | Required, active user |
| Email | Valid email format where provided |
| Phone | Valid organization-defined format where provided |
| Opportunity Value | Zero or greater |
| Probability | Integer between 0 and 100 |
| Expected Close Date | Valid future or current date |
| Pipeline Stage | Valid configured stage |

Validation must occur on both the client and server.

# 24. Error Scenarios

| Scenario | Expected Result |
|----------|-----------------|
| Duplicate lead detected | Warning or merge suggestion according to organization policy |
| Invalid email format | Validation error |
| Opportunity without account | Validation error |
| Lead already converted | Operation rejected |
| Invalid pipeline stage | Validation error |
| Unauthorized lead conversion | 403 Forbidden |
| Opportunity closed | Further stage updates rejected unless reopened |
| Duplicate contact | Warning or validation according to organization policy |
| Missing required follow-up data | Validation error |
| Restricted account access | 403 Forbidden |

Error messages should clearly describe the issue while avoiding disclosure of internal implementation details.
